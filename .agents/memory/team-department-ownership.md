---
name: Team department ownership
description: Durable rule for assigning operational teams to PCC departments.
---

Every team belongs to exactly one official PCC department. Existing teams are classified as Horticulture; newly created teams must explicitly choose a department from the shared official list.

**Why:** Team ownership is required to expand schedules, staffing, reporting, and operational workflows beyond Horticulture without relying on team-name conventions.

**How to apply:** Reuse the shared department values and labels for team validation and selectors. Do not maintain a separate team-only department list.
